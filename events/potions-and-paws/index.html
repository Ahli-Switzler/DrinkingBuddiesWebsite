How It Came Together

This was the very first event Drinking Buddies ever threw: a Halloween-themed cocktail night at Pearl Diver, costumes encouraged for humans and dogs alike, built as an activation for Pawster, a local Nashville nonprofit.

Pawster is more than a one-off partner. Every single copy of Drinking Buddies has a QR code on the back linking to portraits and stories of dogs Pawster supports, often through service workers going through addiction treatment. That connection between hospitality and recovery runs close to home for us.

This wasn't a fundraiser we ran. Drinking Buddies built the whole event and Pearl Diver hosted it, purely to give Pawster visibility: a costume party, cool stuff for the dogs, and a room full of people meeting them for the first time. A nice surprise on top: Doric Lodge 732 F&AM used the night to present Pawster with a $1,000 check of their own, nothing we raised, just a good thing that happened in a room we built.
