How It Came Together

This was the very first event Drinking Buddies ever threw: a Halloween-themed cocktail night at Pearl Diver, costumes encouraged for humans and dogs alike, benefiting Pawster, a local Nashville nonprofit.

Pawster is more than a one-off partner. Every single copy of Drinking Buddies has a QR code on the back linking to portraits and stories of dogs Pawster supports, often through service workers going through addiction treatment. That connection between hospitality and recovery runs close to home for us, and Potions & Paws was our way of putting real money behind it.

Three brand-sponsored cocktails ran the menu that night, the turnout was around 50 people and 15 dogs for a first-ever event, and the outreach it generated made it clear this was worth doing again.
