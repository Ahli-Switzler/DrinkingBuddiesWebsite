The Good Old Fashion Party

A competition for bartenders and their regulars.

Monday, November 16 · 7PM
Barrel Proof Nashville

Let's throw a regular party! The Good Old Fashion Party is a two round competition at Barrel Proof where a bartender competes alongside a regular they picked themselves as teammates.

Round One

An old fashioned, judged entirely by the standard of one person: the regular. Guests taste, guests vote, tokens in jars. The regular is the spec, the room is the jury. Top four pairs advance.

Round Two

The bartender puts their hands behind their back and does not touch a thing. The regular does all of it. Every pour, every stir, every garnish, run entirely on the bartender's voice.
