The Regulars' Ball

Let's throw a regular party! The Regulars' Ball is a two round competition at Barrel Proof where a bartender competes alongside a regular they picked themselves as teammates.

Round One

An old fashioned, judged entirely by the standard of one person: the regular. Guests taste, guests vote, tokens in jars. The regular is the spec, the room is the jury. Top four pairs advance.

Round Two

The bartender puts their hands behind their back and does not touch a thing. The regular does all of it. Every pour, every stir, every garnish, run entirely on the bartender's voice.

Round Two Judges

The regulars who don't make it past round one get entered into a raffle. The more votes your pair got in round one, the more times your regular's name goes in. Whoever gets drawn judges round two, alongside Barrel Proof's own regular.
