The Regulars' Ball

Let's throw a regular party! The Regulars' Ball is a two round competition at Barrel Proof where a bartender competes alongside a regular they picked themselves as teammates. This is an event about the micro communities that bars cultivate, so we're bringing in ten bars from different neighborhoods around Nashville.

The Spirit

Five brands from the Bacardí portfolio, two bars per brand. Every bar gets randomly assigned a spirit, so nobody shows up to find six bars using the same bottle.

Round One

Bartender and regular create a drink with their assigned spirit that represents the bar from the regular's perspective. Both of them are behind the table: the bartender talks about the drink, the regular talks about the inspiration. Guests taste, guests vote. Top four pairs advance.

Round Two: Classics Face Off

The bartender is blindfolded with their hands tied. They get 30 seconds to scan the well and see what they have and where things are. Then each pair gets assigned a classic cocktail, and the regular has 2 minutes to make it while the bartender walks them through it.

Round Two Judges

The regulars who don't make it past round one get entered into a raffle. The more votes your pair got in round one, the more times your regular's name goes in. Whoever gets drawn judges round two, alongside Barrel Proof's own regular. Drinks are scored on taste, time, and teamwork (how the bartender talks their regular through it).

The Prize

Engraved bottles of Angel's Envy for the winners.
